# DRW-003 — Square-to-Round Transition Duct

Material: 1.5 mm mild steel, density 7.85 g/cm3
All dimensions in millimetres. Tolerance +/-0.5 unless noted.

## Datums
- Datum A: flange underside, Z = -4.0
- Datum B/C: duct centrelines, X = 0 and Y = 0

## Form
- Inlet: 200.0 square with R20.0 corners, at Z = 0
- Outlet: diameter 160.0, at Z = 210.0
- Straight collar 30.0 long from Z = 210.0 to Z = 240.0
- Lofted transition between inlet and outlet sections
- Wall thickness 1.5, constant, both ends open

## Flange
- 250.0 square with R20.0 corners, thickness 4.0
- Extends downward from Datum A to Z = 0
- 8 x diameter 9.0 bolt holes, 12.5 in from the flange edge:
  4 at the corners, 4 at the side midpoints

## Acceptance criteria
- Single watertight solid after the flange is fused
- Volume 315073 mm3 +/- 5%
- Bounding box 250.0 x 250.0 x 244.0 (+/-0.1)
- Centre of mass on X = 0 and Y = 0 (+/-0.1)
- Wall thickness constant; shelling, not two lofts subtracted
